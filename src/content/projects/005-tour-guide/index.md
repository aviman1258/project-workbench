description: |-
  www.deodapper.com 
  Tour Guide creates self-drive day trip itineraries from an airport or starting point to a hotel, optimized around your interests and arrival time. The installable web app uses GPS to follow you in the car, narrating each planned stop and interesting landmarks you pass through text-to-speech. It uses Claude to propose attractions grounded in Wikipedia and map data, routes the day with real timings, and runs offline to guide you with narration.
why: I built Deodapper because the friction of arriving in a new city shouldn't mean you miss exploring it. When you land at an airport or start a drive from anywhere, you want to discover something interesting on the way to your hotel, but the planning is scattered—finding stops that match your interests, timing them to your check-in, navigating while spotting things you pass—all across different apps and tabs. I wanted to explore how AI planning, real-time routing, and mobile-first design could turn a travel day into something less logistical and more like having a local friend in the passenger seat, so I stress-tested it on the road to Houston in November 2026.
