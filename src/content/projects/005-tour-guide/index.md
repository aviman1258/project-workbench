name: Deodapper.com
description: Tour Guide creates optimized self-drive day trip itineraries from an airport or starting point to a hotel, building routes around your interests and timed to your arrival. The installable web app uses GPS to follow you in the car, narrating each planned stop and interesting things you pass along the way through text-to-speech. It uses Claude to propose attractions, grounds them with Wikipedia and map data, routes the day with real timings, and then runs offline in drive mode to guide you with narration.
why: I built Deodapper to turn the friction of arriving in a new city into an opportunity for discovery. When you land at an airport or start from wherever you are, you want to explore something interesting on the drive to your hotel, but planning that route—finding stops that match your interests, timing them to your check-in, navigating turn-by-turn while discovering things you pass—is tedious and scattered across tabs. I wanted to explore how AI planning, real-time routing, and mobile-first design could turn a travel day into something less logistical and more like having a local friend in the passenger seat, then stress-test it on the road to Houston in November 2026.
